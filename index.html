Last Updated: 2026

Tuzluk Games built the Flip and Bounce mobile game as an ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Developer & Contact Information

2. Data Collection & Third-Party Services

"Flip and Bounce" does not require user registration or accounts. High scores and language preferences are stored locally on your device.

However, the app uses Google AdMob (and Google User Messaging Platform - UMP) to display advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, app interaction data, and diagnostic/crash information for ad delivery, personalization (subject to your consent), and fraud prevention.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Security

All data transmitted by Google AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. We do not maintain external servers to store personal user data.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data (best score and settings) stays only on your device and is deleted when you reset the score in-app or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's retention policies. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your Android device Settings (Google > Ads) or through the in-app consent dialog. For any privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact: pranqued.att@gmail.com

5. Children's Privacy

This game is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
